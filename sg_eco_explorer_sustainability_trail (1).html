Pillar 1: City in Nature • Biodiversity

Station 1: School Eco-Garden Food Web Lab

Singapore is a "City in Nature". Our school eco-garden supports native pollinators like Olive-backed Sunbirds, Carpenter Bees, and Lime Butterflies!

Ecosystem Population Mix

Balance native flowering host plants, caterpillars/butterflies, and natural bird predators.

Maintain sufficient host plants so caterpillars don't run out of food while allowing sunbirds to keep populations in natural check!

Hands-on Activity: School Eco-Garden Bio-Blitz Quadrat Survey

Conduct a real species inventory using a 1-metre quadrat frame in your school eco-garden.

Step 1: Quadrat Sampling

Place a 1m x 1m PVC pipe square randomly over a grassy or garden patch.

Step 2: Species Inventory

Count and identify macroinvertebrates (ants, earthworms, ladybirds, snails) using a magnifying lens.

Step 3: Diversity Index

Calculate Total Organisms divided by Total Species Types to determine plot health!

Station 2: Smart Hydroponics Eco-Lab

Singapore aims to produce 30% of our nutritional needs locally by 2030 ("30 by 30"). Hydroponics uses 90% less water than traditional soil farming!

Environment Controller

Set the optimal Electrical Conductivity (EC), pH, and LED spectrum to grow healthy Pak Choy greens.

Keep EC between 1.4 - 2.2 mS/cm and pH between 5.8 - 6.5 for leafy greens to prevent nutrient lock-out!

Hands-on Activity: Upcycled Kratky Hydroponic Jar

Build a passive non-electric Kratky hydroponic unit using household plastic bottles!

Step 1: Container Setup

Cut a 1.5L bottle in half. Invert top funnel into bottom reservoir. Fill net pot with clay pebbles or sponge.

Step 2: Hydro Solution

Mix hydroponic MasterBlend / A+B fertilizer into water until EC reaches ~1.6 mS/cm. Maintain an air gap below net cup!

Step 3: Observation Log

Track daily root length growth (cm) and leaf count over 3 weeks in your club science journal.

Hands-on Activity: School Canteen Plate Waste Audit

Conduct a real-world audit during recess with your team! Calculate how much unconsumed food is discarded daily in your school.

Step 1: Collect Data

Weigh leftover food waste from 3 canteen stalls using a digital hanging scale for 3 consecutive days.

Step 2: STEM Math Challenge

Calculate Average Daily Waste = (Day 1 + Day 2 + Day 3) / 3. Multiply by 200 school days for annual mass!

Step 3: Action Campaign

Create poster reminders: "Love Your Food, Waste Zero!" near dish return stations.

Hands-on Activity: Build a 2-Litre Bottle Bio-Filter

Gather recycled materials and build a real water filter column in your school garden!

Materials Needed

Cut 1.5L mineral bottle, cotton wool, crushed activated charcoal, clean coarse sand, small pebbles, muddy pond water.

STEM Testing

Pour 200ml muddy water. Time how long (seconds) it takes to filter and measure filtered volume using a graduated cylinder.

Scientific Thinking

Why is activated charcoal needed even if water looks clear? (Discuss adsorption vs physical filtration!)

Hands-on Activity: Classroom "Vampire Power" Patrol Audit

Inspect your classroom and toilets for standby power loss ("vampire energy") and air-con efficiency settings!

Step 1: Plug & Sensor Check

Locate visual projectors, document cameras, and water coolers left on standby mode over weekends.

Step 2: Air-Con 25°C Sticker Campaign

Use a digital thermometer to measure classroom temperature. Stick prompt labels near air-con remotes: "Set to 25°C for SG Green Plan!"

Step 3: Toilet Light Monitor

Check if toilet lights remain switched on during bright daylight hours. Submit recommendations to switch to motion sensor switches!
